Tide predictions are essential for a variety of activities, from coastal navigation to beach recreation. But how accurate are these predictions? We conducted a three-month study comparing NOAA tide predictions with actual tide measurements at Santa Monica Bay.

## Methodology

For this study, we:

- Collected NOAA tide predictions for Santa Monica Bay (Station 9410840) from January through March 2025
- Installed a pressure sensor at the Santa Monica Pier to record actual water levels at 5-minute intervals
- Compared predicted high and low tide times and heights with measured values
- Analyzed the differences to determine accuracy patterns

## Overall Accuracy

Our findings show that NOAA tide predictions are remarkably accurate under normal weather conditions:

- **Timing accuracy:** High and low tides occurred within ±10 minutes of predicted times in 92% of cases
- **Height accuracy:** Actual tide heights were within ±0.2 feet of predicted heights in 88% of cases

## Factors Affecting Accuracy

We identified several factors that influenced prediction accuracy:

- **Weather conditions:** Strong onshore winds increased water levels by up to 0.5 feet above predictions
- **Atmospheric pressure:** Low pressure systems raised water levels, while high pressure systems lowered them
- **Storm surge:** During the January 15 storm, actual high tide exceeded predictions by 0.8 feet
- **Seasonal variations:** February showed the highest accuracy, while January (storm season) showed the lowest

## Practical Implications

Based on our findings, here are some practical takeaways:

- For recreational beach users, NOAA predictions are more than adequate for planning purposes
- Boaters and coastal navigators should add a safety margin of at least 30 minutes and 0.5 feet during normal conditions
- During storms or unusual weather patterns, expect greater deviations from predictions
- Morning high tides tended to be slightly higher than predicted, while afternoon low tides were often slightly lower

This research confirms that while tide predictions are highly accurate under normal conditions, they should be considered approximations rather than exact values, especially during unusual weather events.
